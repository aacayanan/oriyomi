Yomi means to read.

A dense chapter is a flat, overwhelming sheet. The hard part is not reading it — it is starting, staying oriented inside it, and knowing afterwards whether any of it stuck.

Oriyomi creases the sheet. Structure detection finds the chapters and sections and turns them into folds; each fold is spoken by a neural voice you can follow by ear and by eye. First fold plays while the rest are still generating.

Hearing a chapter and understanding it are not the same thing.

When a fold finishes, oriyomi can ask what stuck: one to eight multiple-choice questions generated from the document you brought, with an explanation under every answer.

It is an enhancement, not a gate. The reader works fully without it — the quiz appears only when a Gemini key is present, and nothing else changes when it is not.
